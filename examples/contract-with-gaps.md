# Freelance Services Agreement

This Freelance Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective as of 1 November 2026 between Golden Gate Studio LLC, 548 Market St, Suite 210, San Francisco, CA 94104 (the "Consultant"), and Lone Star Coffee Company LLC, 1100 Congress Ave, Austin, TX 78701 (the "Client").

## 1. Services

The Consultant will design and build a marketing website for the Client, including up to five page templates, a content management setup and one round of launch support.

## 2. Fees

The Client will pay a fixed fee of Four Thousand Five Hundred US Dollars (US$4,800), plus any applicable sales tax. Additional work requested by the Client will be billed at US$120 per hour.

## 3. Payment terms

The Consultant will invoice 50% on signature and 50% on launch. Each invoice is payable within 15 days of the invoice date (Net 15).

## 4. Intellectual property

All deliverables become the property of the Client once the Consultant has received full payment. Until then, the Consultant retains all rights.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other party's non-public business information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days' written notice. The Client will pay for all work completed up to the termination date.

## 7. Limitation of liability

The Consultant's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Client in the three months before the claim.
