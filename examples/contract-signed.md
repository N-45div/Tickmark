# Consulting Services Agreement

This Consulting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is effective from 1 October 2026 until 31 March 2027, between Golden Gate Studio LLC, 548 Market St, Suite 210, San Francisco, CA 94104 (the "Consultant"), and Summit Outdoor Gear Inc., 1600 Wynkoop St, Denver, CO 80202 (the "Client").

## 1. Services

The Consultant will provide brand strategy and design consulting as requested by the Client.

## 2. Fees

The Client will pay US$150 (One Hundred Fifty US Dollars) per hour for consulting time, billed monthly in arrears.

## 3. Payment terms

Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date (Net 30).

## 4. Intellectual property

Deliverables become the property of the Client on payment of the invoice that covers them.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other party's confidential information private and use it only for this Agreement.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice.

## 7. Limitation of liability

Each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid under this Agreement in the preceding six months.

## 8. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
