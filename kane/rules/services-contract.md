# Services contract: completeness and consistency rules

Plain-English checks for a freelance or small-business services agreement, the kind you
sign before doing work and then invoice against. Kane CLI reads the contract in a real
browser and checks each one. This is a sanity check for missing or contradictory terms,
not legal advice. Edit, add or remove rules freely. Each numbered line is checked.

1. The document is titled as an agreement or contract and states its effective date.
2. Both parties are identified by their full legal name and address.
3. The services or deliverables are described.
4. The fee or rate is stated with its currency. Wherever an amount is written both in words and in figures, the words and the figures state the same amount.
5. The payment terms say when invoices must be paid (for example "within 30 days" or "Net 30").
6. It says how either party can terminate the agreement and how much notice is required.
7. It says who owns the work product or intellectual property, and when ownership passes.
8. It contains a confidentiality clause.
9. It names the governing law (which country's or state's law applies).
10. It limits or allocates liability.
11. There is a signature block for each party with a name, a title and a date line.
12. Party names, amounts and dates are used consistently throughout the document.
