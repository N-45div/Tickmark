# Create an inter-state invoice from the editor

Go to {{baseUrl}}/ .
Type "Acme Retail Pvt Ltd" in the Client name field and "Lower Parel, Mumbai" in the Client address field.
Select "Maharashtra" in the Client state dropdown.
Type "Web design" in the Item 1 description field, "998314" in the Item 1 HSN/SAC field, set Item 1 quantity to 10 and Item 1 rate to 2000.
Verify the live preview shows place of supply "Maharashtra (27)", a single tax line "IGST @ 18%" of ₹3,600.00, no CGST or SGST line, and a grand total of ₹23,600.00.
Verify the amount in words reads "Indian Rupees Twenty Three Thousand Six Hundred Only".
Click "Issue invoice" and verify the confirmation "Issued INV/26-27/0001" appears.
